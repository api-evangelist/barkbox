One self-directed scent-based game your dog can play is puzzling out a snuffle mat . Snuffle mats give dogs an outlet for their desire to sniff, are highly rewarding, and are a great way to occupy them inside, especially when recovering from surgery (always check with your vet to ensure this level of activity is okay), as a distraction during a storm, or when you have company. Dogs experience the world through their nose!
