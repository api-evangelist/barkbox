Your home might feel safe to you—but to a curious dog, it’s full of tempting (and sometimes dangerous) surprises. From loose cords and toxic plants to cabinets that are way too easy to open, everyday household items can pose real risks to your pup. Dog proofing your home doesn’t have to be overwhelming.
