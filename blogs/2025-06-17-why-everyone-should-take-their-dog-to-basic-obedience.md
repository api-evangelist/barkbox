Some pet parents might feel nervous or uncomfortable with the idea of taking their new dog or puppy to obedience classes , but the process will bring you closer together! Regardless of what your training goals are, from strengthening your relationship, to someday competing in agility competitions, obedience training is an important foundation. Obedience training might sound boring, but it’s a lot of fun and a great way to learn about your dog and help the two of you bond.
