For more than a decade, we’ve reimagined what life could look like if we truly put dogs first. This was the mindset that we applied in creating BarkBox, giving dogs the joy of surprise with a monthly delivery of toys and treats designed just for them, then extending that same magic to retail stores. It’s why we launched BARK Air, where dogs fly first.
