The majority of dog breeds can adapt to any environment as long as they’re getting properly exercised. But when you’re living in an apartment, an overactive dog can make the space seems SUPER small. Sometimes it would be nice to come home and not be immediately greeted by an overactive pup that jumps all over you and just wants to PLAY PLAY PLAY.
